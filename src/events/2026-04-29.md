The talk: Beyond the Code: How Developers Communicate Their Way Up. In this talk, our presenter will break down the communication habits that separate strong individual contributors from impactful leaders, architects, and consultants. Learn how to frame ideas, guide discussions, influence without authority, and build trust across teams. Expect real-world patterns, examples, and practical techniques you can start using the next day.
